Clarify terminology for food businesses

New guidance

Review the AI recommendation and supporting evidence to decide whether this proposed guidance should be taken forward.

Priority: High

AI recommendation

Proposed new guidance

Use “food business” as the general term when referring to a business that handles or sells food. Use “food business operator” when referring specifically to the person responsible for the business, and “food establishment” when the context requires referring to the premises.

This is proposed wording for review. The distinctions between these terms need to be validated with relevant subject-matter experts before the guidance is adopted.

Evidence

The analysis identified 184 occurrences across 96 pages, with three main terms used to describe food businesses:

  • “Food business” — 102 occurrences
  • “Food business operator” — 51 occurrences
  • “Food establishment” — 31 occurrences

Why this matters

People may encounter different terms when looking for information about similar activities. Clearer guidance could help content designers use terminology consistently and make content easier for users to understand.

Things to consider

Some terms may have specific legal or technical meanings. Any new guidance would need to preserve those distinctions and explain when each term is appropriate.
